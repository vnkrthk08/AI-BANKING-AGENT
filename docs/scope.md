# Prototype scope and safety

## In scope

An English text simulation of a bank outbound service call about an outdated demo mobile app: disclosure, right-party confirmation, permission, app status, generic update guidance, app-update support case/callback intent, human support routing wording, opt-out wording, fraud concern safe redirection, confidence fallback, and audit events.

## Out of scope

Production calling, identity verification, payments, OTP/PIN/password/CVV handling, balances/statements, credit decisions, real fraud determinations, voice cloning, STT/TTS, WhatsApp, RAG, vector databases, fine-tuning, and regulatory/compliance claims.

Sensitive authentication text is blocked before session transcript/audit storage. The prototype does not claim to erase content from the transport layer, client logs, or infrastructure outside this process. Do not enter real customer information.
